Tape transport, decision mockup

Phase 2 §5. The strip lives inside the app row's glass card, directly under the header, whenever that app's tape is on. Visible collapsed or expanded, independent of the EQ | Effects toggle. Rendered at the real popup width (510pt token; 462pt inside the row card). Amber always means "you are behind live"; blue always means "an action that changes the tape"; the red dot is the record head, fixed at the live edge.

S1 · Tape off

Default for every app. The row is exactly today's row: no strip, no vertical cost. The tape is turned on in the expanded row's new third segment, Tape (shown in S10).

Behind-live is triple-coded: amber gap on the track, amber offset, LIVE flipping from inert label to blue button. Any one of the three reads at a glance on the collapsed row; the user never wonders why the app sounds "wrong". VU meter shows what you hear (transport output, per E19).

Layout ruling and width math

The strip's fixed elements at 462pt inner width: time slot 40 + LIVE pill ≈ 38 + three icon buttons 3×16 + speed chip ≈ 32 (conditional) + record dot 5 + seven 8pt gaps ≈ 56, totalling ≈ 227pt worst case. The scrub bar keeps ≥ 235pt, comfortably above the 180pt floor.

Did not fit on the strip, moved to the Tape panel: the enable toggle + ring length (deliberate, settings-grade decisions), the speed slider (a second slider 8pt below the volume slider is a mis-drag magnet; the chip keeps speed glanceable), Keep pitch (T7), and the labelled save button with its caveat. Nothing was silently shrunk.

Not designed in: jog buttons (ruled out), reverse play (RT supports r < 0; no UI in v1), a menu-bar icon change while behind live (Erik's §5 open question; the triple-coded strip makes it unnecessary in my judgment, revisit after use), FF button (reach live by dragging the thumb to the terminus, pressing LIVE, or speeds > 1×, which auto-pin at the live edge per Q2).
